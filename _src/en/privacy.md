This policy explains how the Association processes personal data through the website semfealumni.gr, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, “GDPR”), Greek Law 4624/2019 and Greek Law 3471/2006 on the protection of privacy in electronic communications.

## In short { #syntoma }

- You can read the whole website without an account. We use no cookies and no advertising, and we do not build profiles of visitors.
- We count visits only as totals. Our code does not store your IP address; only our providers' technical logs keep it, for a few weeks.
- If you create an account or apply for membership, we keep what is needed for the register of members and what you choose to give us.
- **We do not sell your details and we do not give them to anyone for advertising.**
- You can see, correct and delete your details yourself, from the [My account]({{root}}account/) page.

## Who is responsible { #ypeythynos }

The data controller is the **SEMFE Alumni Association** (“the Association”), the non-profit association of the graduates of the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences of the National Technical University of Athens, registered in Greece as <span lang="el">«Σύλλογος Διπλωματούχων Σχολής Εφαρμοσμένων Μαθηματικών και Φυσικών Επιστημών Εθνικού Μετσόβιου Πολυτεχνείου»</span> (in its Statute's own English version, “School of Applied Mathematics and Physical Sciences Graduates Association, National Technical University of Athens”), with its seat in the Municipality of Zografou, Attica, Greece.

For any matter concerning your personal data, write to us at [gradsemfe@gmail.com](mailto:gradsemfe@gmail.com).

The Association has not appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO), because it is not required to (Article 37 GDPR): it does not monitor people systematically on a large scale and it does not process special categories of data.

## Where this applies { #pedio }

This policy applies to the website semfealumni.gr and its English version, to the account and the membership application, to the Members' area and to the e-mails we send you through the website. It does not apply to third-party websites and services that links from here lead to (e.g. LinkedIn, YouTube, PayPal, Revolut), which have their own policies.

## When you are only reading the website { #episkeptes }

No account is needed. For a page to reach you, your browser connects to the servers below, which receive, as with any website, your IP address and technical details of your browser:

- **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc., USA), which hosts the website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, in accordance with its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement){ newtab }.
- **Google Fonts** (Google), from which the fonts are loaded.
- **YouTube** (Google), only on the page that has an embedded video. The video is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). If you play it, [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy){ newtab } applies and YouTube may store information in your browser.
- **Firebase** (Google, from gstatic.com), only when you need it: when you press “Sign in”, when you open the My account, Feedback and problems or Members' area pages, or if you have signed in from this browser before. The What's new and Statistics pages also ask Firebase what has been approved for publication and what the anonymous member statistics are; this happens without cookies and without anything being stored in your browser.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the website properly and securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Cookies and storage in your browser { #cookies }

**We use no cookies.** The website keeps in your browser (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB) only what is strictly necessary for functions you ask for. For these the law does not require consent (Article 4(5) of Greek Law 3471/2006):

- **The language** you chose with the flags at the top (localStorage), so that the next pages open in that language.
- **On the Statistics page, the period you chose** (sessionStorage), so that it stays the same while you look at the page. It is erased as soon as you close the tab.
- **Your sign-in**, if you sign in. Firebase keeps what it needs to keep you signed in (IndexedDB and localStorage). The website keeps your account's name, e-mail address and photo, so that they appear at the top of the page straight away, a note for the account menu (e.g. the status of your application) and small notes for the sign-in to work (e.g. that you pressed “Sign out” or that your e-mail is awaiting confirmation<!--if:linkedin-->, or a one-time code during the LinkedIn sign-in<!--/if:linkedin-->). All of these are erased when you sign out or delete your account.
- **For administrators only**: the draft of an announcement they are writing, so that it is not lost if the page closes.

You can erase them whenever you like from your browser's settings. You will then simply need to sign in or choose your language again.

## Visit statistics { #statistika }

To know how many people read the website and from where, we use two counters. Neither of them stores anything on your device. The results, only as totals, are published on the [Statistics]({{root}}analytics/) page.

- **Google Analytics 4, without cookies.** It stores nothing on your device (no cookie and nothing else), and we have switched off the advertising features and “Google signals”. It sends Google the page you read (without the parameters of its address), the type of your device and the page you came from. From your IP address Google works out the country and the city; according to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Because there is no identifier on your device, it cannot know whether you are the same visitor as yesterday. Google keeps the detailed data for as long as our account's retention setting says, at most 14 months.
- **Our own counter.** Each page sends our server (Google Cloud, in the European Union) a short message: which page you read, whether it is the first page of your visit (that is, whether you did not reach it from another page of the website), and which website you came from. The server also reads your browser's “user agent”, only to tell robots apart and to count the type of device (mobile, tablet, computer). Of the website you came from it keeps only its kind (search engine, social network, e-mail, other website or direct), and it counts the hour of the visit. On the first page of each visit, it looks up which organisation owns the network you are connecting from, by asking the public address registries: the Domain Name System (DNS) and the RIPE NCC registry (the Netherlands), which is responsible for Europe and passes questions about addresses from other regions on to their own registry (ARIN in North America, APNIC, LACNIC or AFRINIC). The registries receive only the address, in order to answer. The server keeps **only the name** of the university or company, if the network belongs to such an organisation. Home and mobile connections belong to internet providers and are not matched to anyone. We keep only totals per day (for example “12 visits to Announcements”, “2 visits from NTUA”), never a record per visitor. A company is shown publicly only if it has at least two visits in the period.

**Our code does not store or log your IP address.** As with every request, Google Cloud's infrastructure keeps a technical log that includes the address, for a limited period (normally up to 30 days). We open it only to fix a problem or to deal with abuse.

If your browser asks not to be tracked (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track), we do not count your visit at all, with either of the two counters. The administration pages and the LinkedIn sign-in return page are never counted.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how many people read the website and from where, so that we can improve it and tell the members about its reach (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The daily totals do not concern any person and we keep them without a time limit.

## Your account { #logariasmos }

Signing in works through Google's **Firebase Authentication** service. You can sign in with {{signin}} and password. If you create an account with e-mail, you give us your name and e-mail address yourself and choose a password, which Firebase stores in a form that cannot be reversed (a hash) and which is not visible to anyone, not even to us. Firebase sends you an e-mail to confirm your address; until you confirm it, the account cannot be used.

<!--if:social-->

If you sign in with {{signin-social}}, we receive only the following from the provider:

- your **name**,
- your **e-mail** address,
- your **profile photo** (if there is one), and
- an identifier of your account with the provider.

We do not receive the password of your {{signin-social}} account, nor any access to your contacts, posts or messages.

<!--/if:social-->
<!--if:linkedin-->

If you sign in with LinkedIn, we keep a record that links your LinkedIn identifier to your account here, so that we recognise you the next time. It is deleted together with your account.

<!--/if:linkedin-->

Firebase also keeps when the account was created and when you last signed in.

The website's administrators see a list of all accounts: name, e-mail, photo, whether the e-mail is confirmed, the ways of signing in, when each one was created and last signed in, and whether it has a membership application. They can download it as a file (CSV). They use it to approve members and to find duplicate accounts of the same person and merge them into one. Every such merge is recorded in a log that only the project's administrators can see in Firebase: which accounts, their names and e-mails, who made the merge and when. We keep this log for security and accountability for as long as the account that was kept exists, and it is deleted together with that account; earlier, if you ask us to, unless it is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Legal basis: providing the service you ask for, under the [terms of use]({{root}}terms/) (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in the security of accounts and in avoiding duplicate registrations (Article 6(1)(f)).

## The membership application { #aitisi }

The Association's register of members is kept on this website. If you fill in the membership application, we store the details you give in Google's **Cloud Firestore** database:

- **Required:** first name, last name, e-mail and role (SEMFE graduate, final-year student or faculty member). Without them we cannot consider the application.
- **Optional:** phone number, years of admission and graduation, study track, employer and position, industry, city, country, gender, LinkedIn profile, a note to the Board of Directors, and your contact preferences (newsletter, job announcements, members' directory). If you do not give them, your application is not affected. The preference boxes are empty until you tick them yourself.
- **What the Board of Directors records:** the status of the application (under review, active, rejected), the reason if it is rejected, which administrator reviewed it and when, and the years for which the membership fee has been paid.

We use them to:

- confirm that you are entitled to become a member, as the [Statute]({{root}}assets/docs/foundation/katastatiko.pdf){ newtab } (PDF, in Greek) provides,
- keep the register of members and the membership fees,
- send you what concerns your membership (e.g. invitations to general assemblies and elections, fee reminders),
- send you the newsletter and announcements of jobs and internships, **only if you choose to**,
- produce the **anonymous** member statistics on the [Statistics]({{root}}analytics/#meli) page.

For the statistics we count only totals per question (e.g. “12 members in Athens”), never two answers of the same person together and never a name or an e-mail. A group of fewer than 3 people is not shown by name (it goes into “Other”), a question is shown only when at least 5 people have answered it, and years are counted in five-year periods. The answer “I prefer not to say” is not counted anywhere. The statistics are recounted every time an application changes or is deleted.

We do not ask for special categories of data (e.g. about health or political or religious beliefs). Please do not write such details in your note or in your messages.

Legal basis: your relationship with the Association as a member, under its Statute (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), for the required details, the register and the fees; your consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for the optional details and the contact preferences, which you can change or erase at any time; and our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) for the anonymous statistics. The box in the application with which you state that you have read this policy only confirms that you have been informed; it is not consent and it is not a legal basis of the processing.

Your details are seen by you and by the members of the Board of Directors who manage the register of members. To manage the register, the administrators can download it as a file (CSV) and copy the addresses of those who chose the newsletter or the job announcements, in order to send them these. **We do not sell them and we do not give them to third parties.**

If your browser does not run JavaScript, the website offers the Association's old online application form on Google Forms. What you fill in there is stored in the Association's Google account, is not linked to an account on the website, and we delete it if you ask us to.

## Membership fees and donations { #pliromes }

Payments are not made on the website but through **PayPal** (PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., Luxembourg) or by bank transfer or through the Revolut app to the account at **Revolut Bank UAB** (Lithuania) shown on the [Membership and Donations]({{root}}support/) page. The website does not see or store card or bank account details. These providers process the payment data as independent controllers, under their own policies.

From each payment we receive the payer's name, the amount, the date, the payment reference and, if you give it, your e-mail address. We use them to record your membership fee in the register of members, to keep the Association's financial records, and, if you give us contact details with a donation, to send you a letter of thanks. The financial reports we publish show only totals of income and expenses and do not name members or donors.

Legal basis: your membership (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), the Association's legal obligation to keep financial and accounting records (Article 6(1)(c)), and our legitimate interest in thanking those who support us (Article 6(1)(f)).

## The members' directory { #katalogos }

If you choose to, a short profile of you (full name, year of graduation, study track, job, city, LinkedIn) appears in the directory of the **Members' area**, which only active members of the Association can see. The members who see it may use it only for communication between members, under the [terms of use]({{root}}terms/). You can withdraw your profile at any time from your account.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

## Alerts, newsletter and job announcements { #email }

If you choose [e-mail alerts]({{root}}account/#alerts) (announcements of the Association, events and meetings, website news), we keep which ones you chose and your account's e-mail address, so that we can send you a short e-mail when something new of the kinds you chose is published. The e-mails go only to your confirmed sign-in address. Every alert e-mail has a link that stops them all with one button, and you can change your choices whenever you like from your account. They are deleted together with your account.

The newsletter and the announcements of jobs and internships are sent by the administrators only to those who chose them in the membership application. If you untick them, they stop from the next mailing.

These e-mails are sent from the Association's e-mail account on Google's Gmail. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 11 of Greek Law 3471/2006). Withdrawing it does not affect what was sent before.

## Feedback, problem reports and correspondence { #sxolia }

If you write to us from the [Feedback and problems]({{root}}feedback/) page, we keep your message, the screenshots you attached, the page it concerns, your browser's technical details (its “user agent”: browser and version, operating system, device) and your account details (identifier, name, sign-in e-mail and whether it is confirmed), so that we can look into it and reply to you. We send you e-mails only at your account's address and only if it is confirmed: a confirmation with the number of your message and, later, our reply.

The messages are seen by the website's administrators, who also receive a copy by e-mail. Every half hour, an automated GitHub job copies them to a private repository on GitHub, where we read them in order to fix the problems. Our answer may be recorded in the website's public repository with the number of your message, never with your name or e-mail.

When you delete your account, your messages are deleted from the database and removed from the copy on GitHub at the next synchronisation (within half an hour). Older versions may remain in the history of the private repository and in the administrators' mailboxes; we delete them if you ask us to. The same applies to e-mails you send us directly: we keep them for as long as we need to reply to you and to have a record of our correspondence, and we delete them if you ask us to, unless they are needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to those who write to us and in improving the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Announcements, photographs and people on the website { #prosopa }

The website publishes information about the Association's activities, which may include personal data: the names of the members of its bodies, the names of speakers and participants in events, photographs and videos from events (in the Photo gallery and in announcements), and documents from its archive (e.g. financial reports signed by members of the Board of Directors).

The legal basis is the Association's legitimate interest in informing its members and the public about its activities, in keeping its history and, for the members of its bodies, in being accountable and transparent about how it is run (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where needed, we ask for consent.

If you appear in a photograph or are mentioned on a page and want to be removed, write to us. We will remove your name or the photograph, or blur your face in it, without undue delay, unless there is a serious reason for it to stay (e.g. a document the Association must keep). Anything published on the internet may already have been copied by third parties (e.g. search engines or web archives), which we do not control.

When an administrator publishes, changes or deletes an announcement from the website, their e-mail address is recorded in the change history of the website's public repository on GitHub.

## Who sees your data { #apodektes }

- **The Board of Directors and the website's administrators**, only as far as their duties require. They must keep the data confidential and use it only for the purposes of this policy.
- **Other active members**, only your profile in the members' directory, if you choose it.
- **The service providers we use**: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Google Analytics, Google Fonts, Gmail) and GitHub (hosting of the website; the automated GitHub Actions jobs which, once a day, read the membership applications to compute the anonymous statistics and, every half hour, copy the messages from the Feedback and problems page; and the private copy of the messages). For the Firebase and Google Cloud services Google acts as a processor on our behalf, under the [Firebase data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms){ newtab }, and for Google Analytics under [Google's data processing terms](https://business.safety.google/adsprocessorterms/){ newtab }. The Association uses Gmail through an ordinary Gmail account, for which there are no data processing terms; Google processes the e-mails there under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **Third parties acting as independent controllers**, when you use them: <!--if:social-->the provider you sign in with ({{signin-social}}), <!--/if:social-->PayPal and Revolut for payments, YouTube for videos, and the public address registries for the visit counter.
- **Public authorities or courts**, only when the law obliges us.

**We do not sell, rent or give your details to third parties for advertising or for any other purpose.**

## Where it is stored and transfers outside the EU { #diavivaseis }

The database (Cloud Firestore) and our server functions (Cloud Functions) are in Google data centres in the European Union. The membership applications and the messages are also read by GitHub's automated jobs, which may run outside the European Economic Area. Some services, such as Firebase Authentication, Google Analytics, Gmail and GitHub, may also process data in countries outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA.

The transfers to Google LLC and GitHub, Inc. in the USA rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795), in which both participate (Article 45 GDPR), and, for the services covered by data processing terms, also on the Commission's standard contractual clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) included in those terms (Article 46 GDPR). We will send you a copy of the safeguards if you ask us.

For visitors connecting from networks outside Europe, the visit counter sends the IP address, through RIPE NCC, to the address registry of their own region (e.g. ARIN in the USA), which receives it only in order to answer and receives no other information about you.

## Legal bases at a glance { #vaseis }

- **Providing the service and membership** (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): the account, the required details of the application, the register of members, the membership fees and communication on Association matters.
- **Consent** (Article 6(1)(a)): the optional details, the members' directory, the alerts, the newsletter and the job announcements. You can withdraw it at any time.
- **Legal obligation** (Article 6(1)(c)): the financial and accounting records, and replying to lawful requests from authorities.
- **Legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f)): the secure operation of the website, the visit statistics, merging duplicate accounts, replying to feedback, informing people about the Association's activities with names and photographs, thanking donors, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. In each case we have weighed that your interests and rights do not override this interest, and you can object (see [Right to object](#enantiosi)).

## How long we keep the data { #diarkeia }

- **Account, membership application, directory, alerts and feedback messages:** for as long as you have an account. They are deleted as soon as you delete it.
- **Financial and accounting records of membership fees and donations:** for as long as accounting and tax legislation requires (normally five years from the end of the year they concern, or longer if the law so requires).
- **Minutes and accounting books of the Association:** what is already recorded in them (e.g. attendance at a general assembly or a vote, a payment) is not deleted, for as long as its operation as an association and the law require.
- **Log of account merges:** for as long as the account that was kept exists (see [Your account](#logariasmos)).
- **Lists the administrators downloaded or copied:** only for as long as they are needed for the purpose they were downloaded for (e.g. a newsletter mailing).
- **Correspondence:** for as long as needed to reply to you and to have a record of our correspondence.
- **Technical logs of the providers:** for a few weeks (at Google Cloud normally up to 30 days).
- **Backups of the providers:** data you deleted may remain in them for a limited period (according to Google, up to about six months) until they are overwritten. They are not used for any other purpose.
- **Anonymous statistics:** without a time limit, since they do not concern any person.
- **Details needed for legal claims:** for as long as the dispute lasts and until the related claims are time-barred.

## Security { #asfaleia }

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data (Article 32 GDPR). For example: all connections are encrypted (HTTPS), Google encrypts the data on its disks, the database's access rules let each person see only what they are entitled to (their own details and, for active members, the directory), passwords are never stored in readable form, and only a few authorised people have administrator access. No system is completely secure; that is why we keep only what is necessary.

If a data breach occurs that may put your rights at risk, we will notify the Hellenic Data Protection Authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and, if the risk is high, we will also inform you without delay (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

## Minors { #anilikoi }

The accounts and the Members' area are intended for graduates, final-year students and faculty members of SEMFE NTUA, that is, for adults. People under 18 must not create an account. If we learn that a minor's account has been created, we will delete it together with its details.

## Automated decisions { #apofaseis }

We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone and we do not profile you (Article 22 GDPR). Membership applications are considered by people, the members of the Board of Directors.

## Your rights { #dikaiomata }

You have the following rights (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR):

- **Access:** to find out whether we process data about you and to get a copy of it.
- **Rectification:** to correct or complete inaccurate or incomplete details.
- **Erasure:** to ask for your data to be deleted (see [Data deletion]({{root}}data-deletion/)).
- **Restriction:** to ask us to stop the processing temporarily, e.g. while we check its accuracy.
- **Portability:** to get the details you gave us in a common, machine-readable format or to ask for them to be sent to someone else.
- **Objection:** see the next section.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what was done before (Article 7(3)).

You can exercise most of them yourself, immediately, from the [My account]({{root}}account/) page. For anything else, write to us at [gradsemfe@gmail.com](mailto:gradsemfe@gmail.com), preferably from your account's address. If we need to make sure that it is you, we will ask only for what is strictly necessary.

We will reply without undue delay and at the latest within one month. If the request is complex or there are many requests, this period may be extended by two further months; we will tell you so within the first month. Exercising your rights is free of charge, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive (Article 12 GDPR). If we cannot grant a request, for example because the law obliges us to keep certain details, we will explain why. The rights are exercised subject to the restrictions provided by the GDPR and Greek Law 4624/2019.

## Right to object { #enantiosi }

**Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest (e.g. visit statistics, photographs and names in announcements, thanks for donations), you have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR).** We will then stop the processing, unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests and rights, or we need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

For the visit statistics it is enough to switch on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser. The e-mails we send you because you asked for them (alerts, newsletter, job announcements) can be stopped at any time, without giving any reason.

## Complaint to the Authority { #katangelia }

If you consider that the processing of your data breaches the law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Hellenic Data Protection Authority** (1-3 Kifisias Avenue, 115 23 Athens, Greece, tel. +30 210 6475600, [complaints@dpa.gr](mailto:complaints@dpa.gr), [www.dpa.gr](https://www.dpa.gr){ newtab }) or with the supervisory authority of the Member State where you live, where you work or where the alleged infringement took place (Article 77 GDPR). We would appreciate it, however, if you wrote to us first, so that we can try to resolve it together.

## Changes to this policy { #allages }

We may change this policy, for example when we add something new to the website or when the law changes. The new version is published on this page with a new date. For significant changes we will also let you know with an announcement on the website or by e-mail. If a change needs your consent, we will ask you for it.

This policy is also available in Greek. If the two texts differ, the Greek text applies.
