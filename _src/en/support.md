## Become a member

To become a member of the Association of SEMFE Graduates and get access to the members-only area of our website, *all* of the following must be true:

</div>
<ol class="steps">
  <li><h3>You are a graduate or final-year student of SEMFE NTUA</h3><p>You must be a <strong>graduate or final-year student</strong> of the <strong>undergraduate</strong> programme of <strong>SEMFE NTUA</strong>, and we must be able to confirm this in some way. (The easiest way for us to do so is an up-to-date LinkedIn profile, as the application explains.) If you are a <strong>SEMFE faculty member</strong>, <a href="{{root}}contact/">contact us</a> from your Gmail address and we will give you access too.</p></li>
  <li>
    <h3>You fill in the membership application</h3>
    <div data-apply-online hidden>
      <p>Create an account with <strong>{{signin}}</strong> and fill in your details in the <strong>membership application</strong>. From the same account you can later see the status of your membership.</p>
      <p><a class="btn btn-dark" href="{{root}}account/#apply">Create an account and apply</a></p>
    </div>
    <div data-apply-legacy>
      <p>Fill in your details on the <a href="https://goo.gl/F3AzdU" target="_blank" rel="noopener">online form</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li><h3>You pay the €10 membership fee</h3><p>You must have paid the sum of <strong>€10</strong> into the Association's account, which is managed by the Treasurer, Konstantinos Stouras (see <a href="#katathesi">“Payment”</a> below). In the “reference” field, please write your <strong>full name</strong>, your <strong>email</strong> and the <strong>year of the fee</strong> you are paying.</p></li>
</ol>

We will check your details promptly and send you an e-mail confirming that your membership of the Association is active.

## Renewing your annual membership fee { #ananeosi style="font-size:1.5rem" }

Registered members renew their membership every year, again with the sum of **€10**. You can renew either online, with a payment into the Association's account (see [“Payment”](#katathesi) below), or in person at an event, one-day conference or assembly of the Association.

</div>
<div>

## Transparency in the Association's transactions and expenses { #diafaneia style="font-size:1.5rem" }

We keep full transparency over the Association's income and expenses, and over the payments from members' fees and donations. Any member who wishes can learn how the Association's financial transactions and expenses are managed. [Financial reports]({{root}}archive/).

## Support the Association

Anyone who is inspired by the aims and work of the Association of SEMFE Graduates, as described in its [Statute]({{root}}assets/docs/foundation/katastatiko.pdf){ newtab } (PDF, in Greek), **whether or not they are a graduate**, can give any amount they wish to support its running.

Payments can be made into the **Association's account** (see [“Payment”](#katathesi)) with the reference **“donation”**. If you include your full name and email with your payment, the Association will send you a letter of thanks.

[Donate with PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/donate/?hosted_button_id=C662GULWRBQ4C){ .btn .btn-primary newtab }

</div>
<div class="card" id="ofeli">

### Members' benefits

Under the Statute, members of the Association:
{ .muted }

- Join an ever-growing “network of SEMFE graduates” of people with top professional experience or high-level research and academic careers. Our members are in regular contact, discussing job opportunities and studies, and sharing advice in general.
- Give employers, SEMFE graduates and HR professionals the chance to look at their profile for employment, strictly if, and only if, the graduate has chosen to allow this.
- Take part in themed workshops and briefings on the job market.
- If they wish, receive a newsletter with the latest news of the Association of SEMFE Graduates.
- If they wish, receive (exclusive) announcements of job and internship opportunities in Greece and abroad for SEMFE graduates. Many of these announcements are confidential and are made only to our members after [personal contact]({{root}}contact/). So you will not find them on any other website (e.g. LinkedIn, kariera.gr, etc.).

{ .checklist }

In addition, under the Statute, members of the Association have the right:
{ .muted style="margin-top:16px" }

- To vote
- To stand for election to the Board and the Audit Committee
- To speak and vote at General Assemblies

{ .checklist }
