## One united network around SEMFE

</div>
<div>

The SEMFE Alumni Association works to widen the professional rights of its members and to strengthen the ties of graduates with one another, with the School and with the wider bodies of society and the state.

We envision a united network of SEMFE graduates, undergraduate students and professors who keep in active contact on scientific and professional matters, adding value to the School and to NTUA.

## What the Association does

The SEMFE Alumni Association, the association of the graduates of the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences of the National Technical University of Athens, is a public-benefit, scientific and broadly research-oriented association, with no profit-making or political character. Its aims are:

</div>
<!-- The statute's eleven aims, opened one at a time like the questions on
     operationsacademia.org. Each title is a short summary; the statute's own
     words are inside, unchanged. Plain <details>: it works without
     JavaScript, and site.js opens them all when the page is printed. -->

```{accordion skopos}
- q: The professional interests of graduates
  a: >-
    To secure, promote and protect the moral, intellectual, financial and, more generally,
    the professional interests of the School's graduates.
  open: true
- q: Solidarity, collegiality and scientific standards
  a: >-
    To develop close bonds of solidarity and collegiality, and to raise the scientific
    standing of the members, without any political aim.
- q: Events for the members and the School
  a: >-
    To organise events that develop and strengthen the ties between the members of the
    Association and the School.
- q: Supporting teaching and research at the School
  a: >-
    To improve and strengthen the teaching and research work, undergraduate and
    postgraduate, carried out at the School, so as to connect its pool of knowledge with the
    current needs of science and technology.
- q: Help for graduates
  a: >-
    To give moral, professional, material and every other kind of help and support to the
    School's graduates.
- q: Digital library and archive
  a: >-
    To set up and run a digital library and archive on the subject of applied mathematical
    and physical sciences.
- q: "Publications: journals, scientific texts, books"
  a: >-
    To publish journals, scientific texts and books on the subject of applied mathematical
    and physical sciences.
- q: Conferences, seminars, workshops and study trips
  a: >-
    To organise conferences, seminars, workshops and study trips that promote research and
    extend know-how in the scientific field of applied mathematical and physical sciences.
- q: Cooperation with authorities, the European Union and international organisations
  a: >-
    To cooperate with the Greek authorities, the European Union and any other international
    organisation, so as to promote the scientific field of the members of the School and of
    the Association.
- q: Research programmes and innovation
  a: >-
    To seek out and take part in national, European and international programmes that
    promote research and innovation in the scientific field of applied mathematical and
    physical sciences.
- q: Cooperation with related associations and unions
  a: >-
    To cooperate with other related scientific and professional associations and unions in
    Greece and abroad.
```
