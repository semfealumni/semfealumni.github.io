You have the right to ask for your personal data to be deleted (Article 17 of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). Here we explain how, what is deleted, and what we keep by way of exception and why.

## Deleting your account yourself (immediately) { #amesa }

1. Sign in and open the [My account]({{root}}account/#delete) page.
2. At the bottom of the page press **Delete account** and confirm.

For security reasons you may be asked to sign in again before the deletion. The deletion is final and cannot be undone.

### What is deleted { #ti-diagrafetai }

The following are deleted immediately and permanently:

- your sign-in account (name, e-mail, profile photo, ways of signing in),
- your membership application and all its details,
- your entry in the members' directory,
- the e-mail alerts you had chosen,
- the log of the merges of your account with other accounts of yours, if there was one,
<!--if:linkedin-->
- the record linking your account to LinkedIn, if there was one,
<!--/if:linkedin-->
- the messages you sent us from the Feedback and problems page, together with the screenshots.

The copy of your messages in our private repository on GitHub is removed at the next synchronisation, within half an hour. The notes the website kept in your browser for signing in are erased too.

The **anonymous member statistics** (e.g. “12 members in Athens”) are recounted automatically, without you, as soon as your application is deleted. The visit statistics are only daily totals; they do not concern any person and do not change.

## Deletion by e-mail { #email }

If you cannot sign in, send an e-mail to [gradsemfe@gmail.com](mailto:gradsemfe@gmail.com?subject=Data%20deletion) with the subject “Data deletion” from your account's address. We will delete your data without undue delay and at the latest within one month of receiving the request, and confirm this to you. If you write from another address, we may ask you for details to make sure that it is you; we will not ask for more than is needed.

In the same way you can ask us to delete what is not deleted automatically, such as the e-mails we have exchanged or an application you submitted on the Association's old online form on Google Forms.

## What we keep by way of exception { #exaireseis }

We keep some details even after deletion: what the law obliges us to keep and what is needed for legal claims (Article 17(3)(b) and (e) GDPR), and, for a limited period, the items below, each with its reason. We keep only what is necessary and only for as long as needed. If you ask us by e-mail to delete your data, we also delete those of them that the law does not require and that we do not need for legal claims.

- **Financial and accounting records** of membership fees and donations, for as long as accounting and tax legislation requires (normally five years from the end of the year they concern).
- **Minutes and accounting books of the Association:** what is already recorded in them (e.g. attendance at a general assembly or a vote, a payment) is not deleted, for as long as its operation as an association and the law require.
- **Lists the administrators downloaded or copied:** anything downloaded to a file or copied to send the newsletter before the deletion is not erased automatically. We keep it only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was downloaded for, and we delete it earlier if you ask us to.
- **Correspondence:** the e-mails we have exchanged remain in the mailbox of the Association and of its administrators, and older versions of your messages may remain in the history of our private repository, so that there is a record of our correspondence. We delete them if you ask us to.
- **Details for legal claims:** what is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, until the dispute ends or the claims are time-barred.
- **Backups and technical logs of our providers** (Google, GitHub): for a limited period, until they are overwritten, without being used for any other purpose.

## Only want to stop something? { #merika }

You do not need to delete your whole account to:

- **stop the e-mail alerts:** press the link at the end of any alert e-mail or change [your choices]({{root}}account/#alerts),
- **stop the newsletter or the job announcements:** untick them in the membership application, from [your account]({{root}}account/). They stop from the next mailing,
- **leave the members' directory:** withdraw your profile from [your account]({{root}}account/),
- **correct or erase a detail:** change it in the membership application,
- **stop us counting your visits:** switch on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser.

## Leaving the Association { #apoxorisi }

The Association's register of members is kept on this website. If you delete your account, your application, the status of your membership and the recorded years of your fee are also deleted from the register. If you want to remain a member, tell the Board of Directors first at [gradsemfe@gmail.com](mailto:gradsemfe@gmail.com). If you also want to leave the Association as a member, let them know as well. Under Article 7 of the [Statute]({{root}}assets/docs/foundation/katastatiko.pdf){ newtab } (PDF, in Greek), contributions already paid are not refunded.

## Removing a photograph or a mention of you { #afairesi }

Photographs from events, names in announcements and documents in the archive are not linked to your account, and so they are not deleted with it. If you appear or are mentioned somewhere on the website and want to be removed, write to us at [gradsemfe@gmail.com](mailto:gradsemfe@gmail.com) with the address of the page. We will remove your name or the photograph, or blur your face in it, without undue delay, unless there is a serious reason for it to stay (e.g. a document the Association must keep), in which case we will explain why.

## If you signed in with {{signin-social}} { #paroxos }

Deleting here deletes the data that we keep. To also remove the app's access from your account with the provider:

<!--if:google-->
- **Google:** [Your Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections){ newtab } → choose the app → Delete all connections.
<!--/if:google-->
<!--if:linkedin-->
- **LinkedIn:** Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services → Remove.
<!--/if:linkedin-->
<!--if:facebook-->
- **Facebook:** Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → choose the Association's app → Remove.
<!--/if:facebook-->

The data the provider itself keeps about you is governed by its own privacy policy.
<!--/if:social-->

## If you are not satisfied { #katangelia }

If you consider that we have not handled your request as the law requires, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the [Hellenic Data Protection Authority](https://www.dpa.gr){ newtab } (1-3 Kifisias Avenue, 115 23 Athens, Greece, tel. +30 210 6475600, [complaints@dpa.gr](mailto:complaints@dpa.gr)), or with the supervisory authority of the Member State where you live, where you work or where the alleged infringement took place (Article 77 GDPR).
