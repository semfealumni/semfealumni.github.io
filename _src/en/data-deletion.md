## Deleting it yourself (immediately)

1. Sign in and open the [My account]({{root}}account/#delete) page.
2. At the bottom of the page press **Delete account** and confirm.

Your sign-in account, your membership application, your entry in the members' directory and the e-mail alerts you had chosen are deleted immediately. For security reasons you may be asked to sign in again before the deletion.

## Deletion by e-mail

If you cannot sign in, send an e-mail to [gradsemfe@gmail.com](mailto:gradsemfe@gmail.com?subject=Data%20deletion) with the subject “Data deletion” from your account's address. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm this to you.

## If you signed in with {{signin-social}}

Deleting here deletes the data that we keep. To also remove the app's access from your account with the provider:

<!--if:google-->
- **Google:** [Your Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections){ newtab } → choose the app → Delete all connections.
<!--/if:google-->
<!--if:linkedin-->
- **LinkedIn:** Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services → Remove.
<!--/if:linkedin-->
<!--if:facebook-->
- **Facebook:** Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → choose the Association's app → Remove.
<!--/if:facebook-->
