Terms of use

The rules for using the website, the accounts and the Members' area.

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Greek text, the Greek text applies.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Who we are

The website semfealumni.gr (“the website”) belongs to and is run by the SEMFE Alumni Association (“the Association”), the non-profit association of the graduates of the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences of the National Technical University of Athens, registered in Greece as «Σύλλογος Διπλωματούχων Σχολής Εφαρμοσμένων Μαθηματικών και Φυσικών Επιστημών Εθνικού Μετσόβιου Πολυτεχνείου» (in its Statute's own English version, “School of Applied Mathematics and Physical Sciences Graduates Association, National Technical University of Athens”), with its seat in the Municipality of Zografou, Attica, Greece. Contact: gradsemfe@gmail.com.

The website provides information about the Association's purposes, bodies, activities and announcements, and allows those who create an account to apply for membership, to contact the Association and, as members, to use the Members' area. Its use is free of charge.

2. Acceptance of the terms

By using the website you accept these terms. When you create an account or sign in, the sign-in window tells you, with a link to this page, that by continuing you accept them. If you do not agree, do not create an account and do not use the website.

How we handle your personal data is described in the privacy policy, which is information provided under the GDPR and not part of this agreement.

3. Statute and membership

Membership and the rights and obligations of members are governed by the Association's Statute (PDF, in Greek), the Greek Civil Code and the decisions of the Association's bodies. These terms concern only the use of the website. If anything here conflicts with the Statute, the Statute applies.

  • An account on the website does not make anyone a member automatically. Under Article 6 of the Statute, graduates of SEMFE NTUA are enrolled as regular members on their application. The Board of Directors decides within fifteen days of the application; if it does not answer within that time, the application is deemed approved. If it refuses, it gives full reasons for its decision, and you may bring the refusal before the General Assembly.
  • If you apply as a final-year student or a faculty member, the Board of Directors decides, in accordance with the Statute, whether you will be enrolled as a member or will only be given access to the Members' area.
  • Access to the Members' area of the website opens when the Board of Directors marks your application as active, once its details have been checked and the membership fee has been paid where required.
  • The details you give in the membership application must be true, accurate and up to date. If they change, update them from your account.
  • The amount of the annual membership fee is set by the Board of Directors and is shown on the Membership and Donations page. Under Article 7 of the Statute, those who leave are not entitled to a refund of their contributions, and members who do not pay their fee for two years are struck off by decision of the Board of Directors.
  • The Association's register of members is kept on this website. If you delete your account, your application, the status of your membership and the recorded years of your fee are also deleted from the register. If you want to remain a member, tell the Board of Directors first at gradsemfe@gmail.com. If you also want to leave the Association, let them know as well.

4. Membership fees and donations

  • Payments are made through third parties (PayPal, a bank or Revolut), under their own terms and charges. The Association is not responsible for errors, delays or charges of the payment providers.
  • Donations are voluntary. Membership fees and donations are not refunded, unless they were paid by mistake (e.g. a double payment or a wrong amount), in which case we refund the amount paid by mistake if you ask us within a reasonable time.
  • The Association does not give tax or other advice about your payments.

5. Accounts

  • You can create an account with Google, LinkedIn or e-mail and password. To create an account you must be at least 18 years old.
  • The account is personal and cannot be transferred. Each person needs one account; if we find two accounts of the same person, we may merge them into one.
  • You are responsible for the security of your account and of your password, and for what is done with it. If you suspect that someone else is using it, change your password and let us know immediately.
  • If you sign in with e-mail, you must confirm your address before using the account.
  • You can delete your account at any time: see Data deletion.

6. The Members' area and the directory

The directory of the Members' area is accessible only to active members and exists only for communication between members. The following are prohibited:

  • copying or exporting it, in whole or in part, by any means, and collecting details from it by automated means,
  • using it for advertising, marketing or mass messages,
  • making it available to third parties or publishing it.

Anyone who uses other members' details for another purpose is personally responsible towards them, and under the GDPR as the controller of their own use.

7. What is not allowed

By using the website you undertake not to:

  • send or post content that is unlawful, defamatory, offensive, threatening, racist or that infringes the rights of others (e.g. intellectual property, personal data, personality),
  • impersonate another person or give false details,
  • gain or attempt to gain access to accounts, data or systems you are not authorised to access, or circumvent security measures,
  • overload the website or introduce viruses or other malicious software,
  • collect data from the website by automated means or create accounts by automated means,
  • use the website for commercial purposes or for sending unsolicited messages,
  • post other people's personal data without a lawful reason, including in the messages and screenshots you send us.

If you find a security vulnerability, let us know at gradsemfe@gmail.com, without exploiting it and without making it public before we fix it.

8. Content you send us

You are responsible for whatever you send us through the website (e.g. the membership application, your profile in the directory, messages and screenshots). You declare that you have the right to send it and that it does not breach the law or the rights of third parties. You grant the Association a free, non-exclusive licence to use it only as far as needed for the purpose for which you sent it (e.g. to look into a problem or to show your profile to members). The Association may refuse or remove content that breaches these terms or the law.

9. Reporting illegal content

If you consider that some content on the website is illegal or breaches these terms, for example because it infringes your intellectual property or your personal data, write to us at gradsemfe@gmail.com with:

  1. the exact address (URL) of the page and the content concerned,
  2. a short explanation of why you consider it illegal,
  3. your name and e-mail (not needed if the report concerns child sexual abuse material), and
  4. a statement that what you report is accurate and that you write in good faith.

We will confirm that we received the report, examine it in a timely, diligent and objective manner, and let you know our decision. We do not use automated means to review or remove content. If we remove or restrict content or an account, we will inform the user affected, explaining the reasons (Article 17 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), and they may ask us to review the decision. This is without prejudice to each party's right to go to court or to the competent authority.

This e-mail address is also our single point of contact for authorities and users under Articles 11 and 12 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 on digital services, where it applies. You can write to us in Greek or in English.

The texts, photographs, videos, documents, logo and design of the website belong to the Association or to their creators and are protected by Greek Law 2121/1993 on intellectual property. The Association's name and logo are its distinctive marks.

You may read, print and share pages or extracts of the website for personal, non-commercial use, with a reference to the source and a link to the page, without altering them and without giving the impression that the Association endorses you. Any other use, and in particular any use of the name or the logo, requires our written permission. When you share photographs in which people appear, respect their rights to their image and their private life.

The Association expressly reserves its right regarding text and data mining of the website (Article 21B of Greek Law 2121/1993, added by Greek Law 4996/2022, which transposes Article 4 of Directive (EU) 2019/790): automated collection of the content, in particular for training artificial intelligence systems, is not allowed without our permission, subject to what the law allows for scientific research. This does not prevent search engines from indexing the website.

The website has links to third-party websites and services (e.g. LinkedIn, YouTube, PayPal, Revolut) and uses third-party services for signing in. These have their own terms and privacy policies. The Association does not control their content or operation and is not responsible for them.

The information on the website is general and informative and does not constitute legal, financial or professional advice. Announcements of jobs, events or opportunities that come from third parties are published to inform the members. The Association does not act as an employment agency, does not check or guarantee their accuracy, and does not take part in your dealings with the third parties they come from.

We take care that the information is accurate and up to date and that the website works properly. The website is, however, provided “as is” and “as available”, without any guarantee that it will always be available, free of errors or without interruptions. We may change, suspend or discontinue any part of it, temporarily or permanently, for example for maintenance or for security reasons.

To the extent permitted by law, the Association is not liable for damage resulting from the use of, or inability to use, the website, from interruptions, errors, viruses or loss of data, from actions of other users or third-party providers, or from third-party content.

This limitation does not apply to damage caused by intent or gross negligence of the Association (Article 332 of the Greek Civil Code), to injury to life, body or health, or in any other case where the law does not allow liability to be limited.

15. Your own liability

You are liable, in accordance with the law, for any damage you cause to the Association, its members or third parties by breaching these terms or the law.

16. Restriction and termination of accounts

The Association may restrict, suspend or deactivate an account, or remove content, if it is used abusively, in breach of these terms or of the law, or if it puts the security of the website or of other users at risk. These measures are proportionate to the seriousness of the breach. We will inform you, explaining the reasons (Article 17 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), unless an authority prohibits us from doing so, and you may ask us to review the decision. Membership is not affected in this way; for membership only the Statute applies.

You can stop using the website and delete your account at any time.

We may change these terms, for example when we add something new to the website or when the law changes. The new version is published on this page with a new date. Minor changes (e.g. corrections of wording or of contact details) apply from their publication. For significant changes we will inform account holders with an announcement on the website or by e-mail, and they will apply from the date we state, at least 15 days after the notice, unless a change is required by law with immediate effect. If you do not agree with the new version, you can delete your account; if you continue to use the website, you accept it.

These terms are governed by Greek law. For any dispute we will first try to find an amicable solution; write to us at gradsemfe@gmail.com. If that is not possible, the courts of Athens have jurisdiction. This does not affect the rights given to you by provisions of law that cannot be set aside by agreement, such as the right of consumers to bring proceedings before the courts of the place where they live.

For any question about these terms, write to us at gradsemfe@gmail.com.
