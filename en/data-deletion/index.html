Data deletion

How to delete your account and all the data we keep about you.

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Greek text, the Greek text applies.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

You have the right to ask for your personal data to be deleted (Article 17 of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). Here we explain how, what is deleted, and what we keep by way of exception because the law requires it.

Deleting it yourself (immediately)

  1. Sign in and open the My account page.
  2. At the bottom of the page press Delete account and confirm.

For security reasons you may be asked to sign in again before the deletion. The deletion is final and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

The following are deleted immediately and permanently:

  • your sign-in account (name, e-mail, profile photo, ways of signing in),
  • your membership application and all its details,
  • your entry in the members' directory,
  • the e-mail alerts you had chosen,
  • the record linking your account to LinkedIn, if there was one,
  • the messages you sent us from the Feedback and problems page, together with the screenshots.

The copy of your messages in our private repository on GitHub is removed at the next synchronisation, within a short time.

Deletion by e-mail

If you cannot sign in, send an e-mail to gradsemfe@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion” from your account's address. We will delete your data without undue delay and at the latest within one month of receiving the request, and confirm this to you. If you write from another address, we may ask you for details to make sure that it is you; we will not ask for more than is needed.

In the same way you can ask us to delete what is not deleted automatically, such as the e-mails we have exchanged.

What we keep by way of exception

The law (Article 17(3) GDPR) allows, and in some cases requires, us to keep certain details even after deletion. We keep only what is necessary and only for as long as needed:

  • Financial and accounting records of membership fees and donations, for as long as accounting and tax legislation requires (normally five years from the end of the year they concern).
  • Register of members: if you have been a member, the Association may keep only the minimum details that prove your membership and your participation in its bodies and votes (e.g. full name and period of membership), for as long as its operation as an association requires.
  • Log of account merges: if your account was ever merged with another, the record of it (which accounts, names, e-mails, when) remains for security and accountability, until it is no longer needed or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Correspondence: the e-mails we have exchanged remain in the mailbox of the Association and of its administrators, and older versions of your messages may remain in the history of our private repository. We delete them if you ask us to.
  • Details for legal claims: what is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, until the dispute ends or the claims are time-barred.
  • Backups and technical logs of our providers (Google, GitHub): for a limited period, until they are overwritten, without being used for any other purpose.

The anonymous statistics (e.g. “12 members in Athens”) do not concern any person and do not change when you delete your account.

Only want to stop something?

You do not need to delete your whole account to:

  • stop the e-mail alerts: press the link at the end of any alert e-mail or change your choices,
  • stop the newsletter or the job announcements: untick them in the membership application, from your account,
  • leave the members' directory: withdraw your profile from your account,
  • correct or erase a detail: change it in the membership application,
  • stop us counting your visits: switch on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser.

Leaving the Association

Deleting your account deletes your details from the website. If you also want to leave the Association as a member, let the Board of Directors know at gradsemfe@gmail.com. Under Article 7 of the Statute (PDF, in Greek), contributions already paid are not refunded.

Removing a photograph or a mention of you

Photographs from events, names in announcements and documents in the archive are not linked to your account, and so they are not deleted with it. If you appear or are mentioned somewhere on the website and want to be removed, write to us at gradsemfe@gmail.com with the address of the page. We will remove or blur the photograph or your name without undue delay, unless there is a serious reason for it to stay (e.g. a document the Association must keep), in which case we will explain why.

If you signed in with Google or LinkedIn

Deleting here deletes the data that we keep. To also remove the app's access from your account with the provider:

  • Google: Your Google Account connections → choose the app → Delete all connections.
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services → Remove.

The data the provider itself keeps about you is governed by its own privacy policy.

If you are not satisfied

If you consider that we have not handled your request as the law requires, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (1-3 Kifisias Avenue, 115 23 Athens, Greece, tel. +30 210 6475600).

See also the privacy policy and the terms of use.
